Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Belter ("we", "us", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information, and the third-party AI services we share data with to create your content. By using Belter, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service: (a) account details from Sign in with Apple (name and email, provided by Apple on first sign-in); (b) the content you submit for generation — text prompts, lyrics, songs you create, and voice recordings — which you provide directly in the app; and (c) usage and purchase information such as your credit balance and in-app purchases. We do not collect data from other sources and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use your data

We use your data only to: generate the music, cover art, and voices you request; operate your account, subscription, and credit balance; provide support; and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party AI services we share data with

Belter cannot generate music on your device alone. To produce your content, we send the material you provide over an encrypted connection to specialist third-party AI companies, which create the result and return it to us. We share the minimum needed, and only for the feature you asked for:

  • An AI music and lyrics generation company — receives the description or lyrics you type, and the songs generated from them, to write lyrics, compose and perform the track, and, if you use the stems feature, separate it into individual parts.
  • An AI voice company — receives your voice recording, only if you choose to clone your voice, to build a singing voice model you can reuse on your own tracks.
  • An AI image generation company — receives your song's title and chosen style, to create the cover artwork. It never receives your lyrics or your photos.
  • A cloud hosting provider — operates our backend, database, file storage and authentication.

All of these companies act as our processors under written agreement. They may use your data only to provide their service to us. They may not use it for their own advertising, may not sell it, and may not use it to train general-purpose AI models. Each is contractually required to protect your data to a standard at least equivalent to this policy.

We ask for your permission inside the app, and show you exactly what will be sent and why, before anything is shared. If you decline, no content is sent and the generation features remain switched off. You can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

4. Voice data

Voice recordings are treated as sensitive. Your recording is processed to build a voice model you can reuse on your own tracks. We do not use your recordings to train general-purpose AI models, and you can delete your custom voices at any time. Photos you choose as cover art stay on your device.

5. Public sharing & the community feed

Belter includes a public community feed (Explore). Sharing is off by default and only happens if you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the tracks you create are published to the feed along with their title, cover art, audio, and the display name on your account — these become visible to all other users of the app, so please do not put personal information in titles or lyrics you intend to share. We also record how many times a shared track is played. Turning sharing off in Settings stops future tracks from being published; to remove something already published, contact us. Reports you file and creators you block are stored so we can act on them and keep your feed filtered.

6. Data retention

We keep your content and account data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can delete individual items in the app, and you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Belter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will update the date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app where appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@belterai.app.
